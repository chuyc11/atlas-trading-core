# A-Share Candidate Interpretation

v0.7.5 candidates are research candidates for the strict tradable A-share universe.

They are generated from v0.7.4 score tables and are inputs for future research review, virtual portfolio construction, and daily owner briefing. They are not investment advice, not buy/sell signals, not portfolio weights, not order instructions, and not a profit guarantee.

## How To Read Candidate Pools

- Long candidates are 6-24 month research candidates.
- Mid candidates are 1-6 month research candidates.
- Short candidates are 5-20 trading-day research candidates.
- Extended watch pool keeps a broader per-horizon review set for future analysis.
- Multi-horizon candidates highlight overlap across horizons or strong composite percentile.
- Risk-downgraded candidates identify high-scoring names that failed risk, liquidity, or confidence checks.

## Explanations

Each strict candidate includes:

- primary inclusion reasons
- main risk reasons
- component highlights
- confidence notes
- source score manifest reference
- explicit disclaimer flags

Reason lists explain why a symbol entered a research pool. They do not authorize trading.

## What Candidates Do Not Mean

Candidates do not mean:

- a buy recommendation
- a sell recommendation
- an expected profit
- a target weight
- a position size
- an order plan
- broker readiness
- live-trading readiness
