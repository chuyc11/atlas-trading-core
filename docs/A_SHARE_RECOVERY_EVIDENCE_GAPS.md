# A Share Recovery Evidence Gaps

v0.8.18 records missing recovery evidence as explicit gaps and remaining blockers.

Primary artifacts:

- `data/equity_owner_recovery_evidence/daily/2026-06-26/evidence_gap_register.json`
- `data/equity_owner_recovery_evidence/daily/2026-06-26/remaining_blocker_register.json`

Current categories include:

- missing recovery evidence
- missing owner follow-up evidence
- readiness score gap
- threshold preservation required
- waiver not allowed

These gaps block evidence-backed gate prep. They do not authorize public network refresh, full research run, remediation execution, external notifications, broker access, order previews, or orders.
