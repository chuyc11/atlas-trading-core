# A-Share Briefing Interpretation

v0.7.7 briefings are owner-facing daily information summaries.

They summarize existing candidates, virtual portfolios, industry exposure, risk/liquidity status, and audit state. They do not create new scores, new candidates, or new virtual portfolio weights.

## How To Read The Briefing

- Candidate Top 10 tables show relative research candidates, not trading advice.
- Multi-horizon candidates show cross-horizon score overlap, not certainty.
- Risk-downgraded candidates show names that scored well but failed risk, liquidity, or confidence gates.
- Virtual portfolio summaries show research tracking portfolios, not real portfolios.
- Industry exposure highlights concentration and data-quality warnings.
- Risk/liquidity notes should be read with the same weight as candidate summaries.

## What Not To Misread

- A candidate is not a buy instruction.
- A virtual target weight is not an order instruction.
- A briefing is not a broker workflow.
- A high score is not a profit guarantee.
- Current v0.7.7 artifacts have no real-trading validation.
